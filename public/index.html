Questions

Is Trap Picks a sportsbook?

No. We sell information. We never take bets or hold money for wagers, and pick'em groups are for bragging rights only, with no prizes.

Do the picks always win?

No. TRAP calls have won about 54% since 2016, and the standard -110 odds need 52.4% to break even. Expect losing weeks. Bet only what you can afford to lose.

How do I sign up?

Tap Continue with Google, or enter any email address (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook or another) and we send you a one-tap sign-in link. There is no password to remember.

How does paying work?

Checkout is run by Stripe, so we never see your card. Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your plan renews automatically, and you can cancel or switch plans anytime from your account.

Where does the data come from?

Lines and results come from the free nflverse data, injuries from the official NFL report, live scores from ESPN and forecasts from Open-Meteo. The data is checked before every update.
